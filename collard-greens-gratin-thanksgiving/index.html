Smoky Collard Greens Gratin (Thanksgiving for 5)

The tested recipe scaled up by half for Thanksgiving, nothing else changed. Smoked Gouda takes the place of the ham hock collards usually cook with, and crispy shallots and sourdough crumbs go on top.

6 servingsAbout 20 g protein per servingPrep 30 minCook 75 minVegetarianGinger-freeMake-aheadHoliday

Ingredients

  • 3 shallots (topping)
  • 70 ml extra virgin olive oil (topping)
  • 60 g sourdough bread (topping)
  • Pinch of kosher salt (topping)
  • 1 kg collard greens, 3 bunches (gratin)
  • Kosher salt (gratin)
  • 45 g unsalted butter (gratin)
  • 1 medium yellow onion, about 180 g (gratin)
  • 5 small garlic cloves (gratin)
  • 12 g flour, 1½ tbsp (gratin)
  • 360 ml whole milk (gratin)
  • 180 ml half-and-half (gratin)
  • 130 g smoked Gouda (gratin)
  • 130 g Gruyère, divided (gratin)
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika (gratin)
  • ¼ tsp plus a pinch cayenne (gratin)
  • ¾ tsp fresh thyme leaves (gratin)
  • Nutmeg, a few gratings (gratin)
  • Black pepper (gratin)
  • 7 ml apple cider vinegar, 1½ tsp (gratin)

Steps

  1. PrepStrip the collard leaves from the stems, roll them up, slice into 2.5 cm ribbons and wash well. Slice the shallots into thin rings. Tear the bread and pulse it into coarse crumbs. Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Grate both cheeses and keep about 30 g of the Gruyère aside for the top.
  2. Blanch the greensBoil the collards in a large pot of well-salted water for 12 to 15 minutes, until tender. Work in two batches if the pot is crowded. Drain, cool a little and squeeze out as much water as you can.
  3. ToppingCook the shallots in the oil in a small skillet over medium heat, stirring, for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden and crisp. Lift onto a paper towel. Toast the crumbs in the leftover oil for 3 to 4 minutes, until golden. Salt both. Once cool, store them separately, airtight, at room temperature.
  4. SauceIn a 28 cm oven-safe skillet, melt the butter over medium. Cook the onion for about 6 minutes, until soft, add the garlic for 1 minute, then stir in the flour for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and half-and-half and simmer, stirring, for 6 to 7 minutes, until slightly thickened.
  5. SeasonOff the heat, stir in the Gouda, the larger portion of Gruyère, the paprika, cayenne, thyme, nutmeg, pepper and vinegar. Taste for salt.
  6. AssembleFold in the greens, spread evenly and top with the reserved Gruyère. Cool, cover and refrigerate.
  7. Bake (Thursday)Take it out of the fridge about an hour ahead. Bake uncovered at 400°F for about 30 minutes (35 if straight from the fridge), until bubbling and browned at the edges. Scatter the crumbs and shallots over the top just before serving so they stay crisp.

Notes

Make ahead: steps 1 to 6 on Wednesday; the assembled gratin keeps a day in the fridge. Step 7 on Thursday. If the shallots soften overnight, warm them on a small tray in the oven for 2 minutes.

Swaps (only if needed; don't buy both): smoked cheddar instead of smoked Gouda, same amount.

Pan: no 28 cm oven-safe skillet? Make the sauce in a wide pot, then fold in the greens and spread it in a 28 x 18 cm (11 x 7 in) baking dish.

Standouts: calcium, vitamin K and folate.
