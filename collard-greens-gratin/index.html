Smoky Collard Greens Gratin

Smoked Gouda takes the place of the ham hock collards usually cook with, and crispy shallots and sourdough crumbs go on top. Lighter than a classic gratin, with whole milk and a splash of cider vinegar.

4 servingsAbout 20 g protein per servingPrep 15 minCook 45 minVegetarianGinger-freeMake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 2 shallots (topping)
  • 45 ml extra virgin olive oil (topping)
  • 40 g sourdough bread (topping)
  • Pinch of kosher salt (topping)
  • 680 g collard greens, 2 bunches (gratin)
  • Kosher salt (gratin)
  • 30 g unsalted butter (gratin)
  • 1 small yellow onion, about 120 g (gratin)
  • 3 garlic cloves (gratin)
  • 8 g flour, 1 tbsp (gratin)
  • 240 ml whole milk (gratin)
  • 120 ml half-and-half (gratin)
  • 85 g smoked Gouda (gratin)
  • 85 g Gruyère, divided (gratin)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (gratin)
  • ¼ tsp cayenne (gratin)
  • ½ tsp fresh thyme leaves (gratin)
  • Nutmeg, a few gratings (gratin)
  • Black pepper (gratin)
  • 5 ml apple cider vinegar (gratin)

Steps

  1. PrepStrip the collard leaves from the stems, roll them up, slice into 2.5 cm ribbons and wash well. Slice the shallots into thin rings. Tear the bread and pulse it into coarse crumbs. Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Grate both cheeses and keep a quarter of the Gruyère aside for the top.
  2. Blanch the greensHeat the oven to 400°F. Boil the collards in well-salted water until tender, 12 to 15 minutes. Drain, cool a little and squeeze out as much water as you can.
  3. ToppingCook the shallots in the oil in a small skillet over medium heat, stirring, until golden and crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Lift onto a paper towel. Toast the crumbs in the leftover oil until golden, 3 to 4 minutes. Salt both.
  4. SauceIn a 20 to 23 cm oven-safe skillet, melt the butter over medium. Cook the onion until soft, about 6 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then stir in the flour for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and half-and-half and simmer, stirring, until slightly thickened, about 5 minutes.
  5. SeasonOff the heat, stir in the Gouda, the larger portion of Gruyère, the paprika, cayenne, thyme, nutmeg, pepper and vinegar. Taste for salt.
  6. AssembleFold in the greens, spread evenly and top with the reserved Gruyère. It keeps in the fridge for a day at this point.
  7. BakeBake until bubbling and browned at the edges, 15 to 20 minutes (25 minutes if chilled). Scatter the crumbs and shallots over the top just before serving so they stay crisp.

Notes

Swaps (only if needed; don't buy both): smoked cheddar instead of smoked Gouda, same amount.

Standouts: calcium, vitamin K and folate.

Test notes: Is there enough smokiness, or does it need more paprika? Is the sauce loose or tight after baking?
