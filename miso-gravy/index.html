Miso Gravy (Triple Batch)

The gravy from the Miso-Chestnut Mushroom Wellington, tripled so there is enough for the Wellington, biscuits and turkey, plus leftovers.

About 1.1 litersAbout About 1 g per 100 ml protein per servingPrep 10 minCook 15 minVegetarianMake-aheadHoliday

Ingredients

  • 90 g unsalted butter
  • 3 shallots
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 48 g flour, 6 tbsp
  • 1.1 liters low-sodium vegetable stock, plus a splash for reheating
  • 1 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 45 ml dry sherry

Steps

  1. PrepFinely chop the shallots and mince the garlic. Strip the thyme leaves. Whisk the miso into the sherry so it dissolves without lumps.
  2. RouxMelt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Cook the shallots and garlic for 2 to 3 minutes, until soft. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  3. StockSlowly whisk in the stock, a ladle at a time at first so no lumps form. Bring to a simmer and cook, whisking now and then, for 8 to 10 minutes, until it coats a spoon.
  4. FinishOff the heat, whisk in the miso-sherry mix and the thyme. Taste; the miso brings enough salt for most people.
  5. ReheatTo serve after chilling, rewarm over low heat for about 10 minutes, whisking in a splash of stock, since it thickens a lot in the fridge.

Notes

Swaps (only if needed; don't buy both): port instead of dry sherry, same amount.

Make ahead: keeps 4 days in the fridge, or freeze for up to 3 months. Whisk well when reheating.

Shopping: one 32 oz (946 ml) carton of stock isn't enough. Buy two cartons, or a 1.5 liter one.

Ginger check: read the labels on the miso and the vegetable stock.
